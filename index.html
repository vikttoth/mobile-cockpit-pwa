EN — Standing cost notice

Sessions you create or approve here are executed on your laptop by the cursor-agent CLI against Cursor’s cloud API (CURSOR_API_KEY). Billing follows your Cursor subscription and model / API usage — see the official pricing page for numbers.

Prompts and outputs are mirrored to OneDrive (cursor-cockpit/state.json). Do not put salary, standby, customer code, or other sensitive Nokia data in prompts.
